Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective immediately upon download

The short version: Your financial data lives on your iPhone. We don't collect it, store it, sell it, or share it. No account required. No tracking.

1. Who we are

Spendwise AI is an iOS personal finance application available on the Apple App Store. For privacy-related questions, contact us at: spendwiseai@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

All your financial data is stored locally on your iPhone. This includes:

  • Expense records (amount, category, merchant, date, notes, mood)
  • Income records
  • Trip expense data and exchange rates
  • Custom categories and merchant names
  • Budget limits and settings
  • Receipt images (stored in the app's private documents folder)
  • AI usage counters (tracked locally for free tier limits)

None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party except as described in Section 4.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your financial data or transaction history
  • Device location
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Photos or camera roll (camera access is only used to scan receipts — images are never uploaded)
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers

4. AI features and data processing

When you use AI features (receipt scanning, bank import, AI chat, insights), the following is sent to our secure backend:

  • Receipt scanning: OCR text extracted from your receipt (not the image)
  • Bank import: Transaction text extracted from your PDF, CSV, or photo
  • AI Chat: A compact summary of your expenses (category totals only — not individual transactions) plus your question
  • Insights & forecast: Aggregated monthly totals by category only

This data is sent over HTTPS to our backend which calls Anthropic's Claude API and returns only the text response. We do not log or store this data beyond what is necessary to serve the response. Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy

5. Permissions we request

  • Camera: Used only to scan receipts. Never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to import a gallery image.
  • Microphone: Used only for voice expense entry via Apple's Speech framework. Audio never sent to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: Used for app lock. Biometric data never leaves your device.

6. PIN and security

Your PIN is stored in the iPhone Keychain using SHA-256 hashing. The raw PIN is never stored anywhere. Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework.

7. iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, your Spendwise AI data will be included in your standard iPhone backup, protected by Apple's encryption. We have no access to your iCloud backup.

8. Children's privacy

Spendwise AI is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA under GDPR). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Your data is on your device — view it in the app at any time.
  • Right to erasure: Settings → Reset All Data within the app, or delete the app from your iPhone.
  • Right to portability: Export all data to Excel via the Summary screen at any time.
  • Right to object: You can disable AI features entirely by not using AI Chat, Scan, or Import. No data is sent to our backend unless you actively use these features.

10. Data retention

We retain no personal data on our servers. The only data we hold:

  • An anonymous device UUID (not linked to any personal information) used for AI rate limiting
  • Temporarily cached AI responses (maximum 7 days) containing no personal information

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.

Privacy questions?

Contact us at spendwiseai@gmail.com — we respond within 48 hours.
