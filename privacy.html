Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applies to Spendwise AI for iPhone (iOS 16+)

The short version: Your financial data lives on your iPhone. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or store it on our servers. AI features send the minimum necessary data to process your request — nothing more.

🚫 We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Who we are

Spendwise AI is an iOS personal finance application available on the Apple App Store. For privacy questions, contact us at spendwiseai@gmail.com or visit our support page at spendwiseai.github.io.

2. Data you enter — stored on your device only

All financial data you enter is stored locally on your iPhone using the device's file system. This includes:

  • Expense records — amount, category, merchant, date, notes, mood tag, payment mode
  • Income records — amount, source, description, recurring status
  • Receipt images — stored in the app's private documents folder, never uploaded
  • Trip expense data and cached exchange rates
  • Custom categories and merchant names you create
  • Budget limits and all app settings
  • Your PIN — stored in the iPhone Keychain as a SHA-256 hash. The raw PIN is never stored anywhere.

None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party except as described in Section 3 below.

3. AI features — what is sent and how it is processed

Spendwise AI uses AI features powered by Anthropic Claude, accessed through our own secure backend proxy hosted on Supabase. When you use an AI feature, the following data is sent over HTTPS to our proxy, which forwards it to Anthropic and returns only the text response:

Our Supabase backend does not log, store, or analyse the content of your requests beyond what is necessary to serve the response. Anthropic processes this data under their own privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy

4. Data Apple collects — App Store & In-App Purchases

Spendwise AI offers a Pro subscription (unlimited AI calls) via Apple In-App Purchase / StoreKit. When you purchase a subscription:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple — we never see your payment card, bank account, or billing details
  • Apple verifies your subscription and notifies our backend of your entitlement status
  • We receive only: subscription product ID and expiry date — no payment information

Apple's data practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: apple.com/privacy

6. Permissions we request

  • Camera: Used only to scan receipts or bank statements. Photos are processed on-device by Apple Vision — never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to import an image from your gallery.
  • Microphone: Used only for voice expense entry via Apple's Speech Recognition framework. Audio is processed on-device and never sent to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: Used for app lock security. Biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework.

All permissions are optional and can be revoked at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security.

7. Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your payment card or bank account details
  • Your browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking data
  • Crash logs or diagnostics (no third-party SDK)

8. Data retention

We retain no personal data on our servers. The only data we hold:

  • An anonymous device UUID (not linked to your Apple ID or any personal information) used solely for AI rate limiting
  • Temporarily cached AI responses (maximum 7 days) to reduce repeat API calls — these contain no personal information
  • Subscription entitlement status from Apple (product ID + expiry only)

9. iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, your Spendwise AI data is included in your standard iPhone backup, protected by Apple's encryption. We have no access to your iCloud backup.

10. Children's privacy

Spendwise AI is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA under GDPR). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

11. Your rights under GDPR (EU / EEA users)

  • Right to access: Your data is on your device — view it directly in the app at any time.
  • Right to erasure: Go to Settings → Reset All Data within the app, or delete the app from your iPhone. This permanently removes all data from your device.
  • Right to portability: Export all your data to Excel at any time from the Summary screen.
  • Right to object: AI features are entirely optional. If you do not use AI Chat, Scan, or Import, no data is ever sent to our backend.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For any GDPR requests, email spendwiseai@gmail.com. Since we do not store personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled directly within the app.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
